Welcome to Autocomplete Bias

A research tool by TH Köln that highlights potential bias in Google Search autocomplete suggestions.

🎨
Bias Highlighting

Autocomplete suggestions are color-coded based on their bias score — from blue to red in 3 intensity levels.

🔬
Research Purpose

This extension is part of a PhD research project studying bias in search engine suggestions.

🔒
Privacy First

No personal data is collected. All data is fully anonymous. You control everything.

📋

Data Collection (Optional)

To improve our research, we can optionally collect anonymized search data. This is entirely optional — the extension works fully without it.

What we collect (if you opt in):

  • The search terms you type into Google
  • The autocomplete suggestions Google shows you
  • The bias scores assigned to each suggestion
  • Timestamp and Google domain used (e.g. google.de)
  • Your browser language setting

What we never collect:

  • Your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • Your IP address
  • Your browsing history outside Google Search
  • Cookies, passwords, or form data
  • Any data that could identify you personally

How it works:

  • Data is stored locally on your device
  • Every hour, buffered data is sent to our secure research server via HTTPS
  • Uploads are completely anonymous — no user IDs, no tracking
  • You can delete all local data at any time via the extension popup
  • You can withdraw consent at any time — just toggle the switch off

By opting in, you consent to the processing of anonymized search data as described above, in accordance with the Privacy Policy and GDPR Art. 6(1)(a).
