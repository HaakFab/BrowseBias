1. Description of Service

Autocomplete Bias ("the Extension") is a Google Chrome browser extension developed as part of academic research at TH Köln (Technische Hochschule Köln). The Extension:

  • Modifies the visual appearance of Google Search autocomplete suggestions by adding color-coded bias indicators.
  • Optionally, and only with your explicit consent, collects anonymized search data for academic research purposes.

The Extension does not alter, filter, reorder, or suppress search results or autocomplete suggestions. It only adds a visual overlay to existing suggestions.
