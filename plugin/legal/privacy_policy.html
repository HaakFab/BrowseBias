2. Purpose and Scope

Autocomplete Bias is a Google Chrome browser extension that visually highlights autocomplete suggestions on Google Search with color-coded bias indicators. Its purpose is to support academic research into bias in search engine query suggestions.

The extension has two functions:

  • Bias Highlighting (always active): Colors autocomplete suggestions based on a bias score. This feature operates entirely within your browser and sends no data anywhere.
  • Data Collection (optional, opt-in): If you consent, the extension collects anonymized search data for research purposes.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 If you opt OUT of data collection (default)

We collect no data whatsoever. The extension operates entirely locally within your browser. No information is transmitted to any server.

3.2 If you opt IN to data collection

We collect the following anonymized data:

Data TypeExamplePurpose
Search query text "climate change" Analyze which queries produce biased suggestions
Autocomplete suggestions ["climate change facts", "climate change hoax", ...] Measure bias in Google's autocomplete
Bias scores assigned "red-high", "blue-low" Evaluate bias scoring accuracy
Timestamp "2026-08-19T10:30:00Z" Analyze temporal patterns
Google domain "www.google.de" Compare bias across regional Google versions
Browser language "de-DE" Analyze language-related bias patterns

3.3 Data we NEVER collect

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your IP address (not logged by the extension or server)
  • Any unique user or device identifiers
  • Your browsing history (we only process Google Search autocomplete)
  • Cookies, passwords, or form data
  • Any data from websites other than Google Search
  • Search results or pages you visit after searching
Complete anonymity: We do not generate, store, or transmit any user identifiers. There is no technical mechanism to link uploaded data entries to a specific person or device. Each upload batch is independent and cannot be correlated with previous or future uploads from the same user.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

The legal basis for processing your data is your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

Consent is obtained through the extension's onboarding flow when you first install the extension. You can choose to opt in or opt out. Data collection only begins after you have actively consented by clicking "Yes, I consent to data collection."

5. How We Process and Store Data

  • Local buffering: Collected data is stored temporarily in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Transmission: Every 60 minutes, buffered data is transmitted to our research server via an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Server storage: Data is stored on a secured research server operated by or on behalf of TH Köln within the European Economic Area (EEA).
  • Access: Only the research team at TH Köln has access to the collected data.
  • No third-party sharing: Data is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third parties.

6. Data Retention

Collected data will be retained for the duration of the research project, estimated at up to 5 years from the date of collection. After the research project concludes, data will either be deleted or further anonymized and archived in accordance with academic data management policies.

Local data (buffered on your device) is deleted automatically after successful upload, or at any time by you using the "Delete My Data" button in the extension popup.

7. Data Transfer

Your data is processed and stored within the European Economic Area (EEA). We do not transfer your data outside the EEA. If this changes in the future, we will update this privacy policy and ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g., EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): You have the right to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").

  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

However, you can always:

  • Withdraw consent and stop future data collection by toggling the switch in the popup.
  • Uninstall the extension, which removes all local data.

11. Withdrawal of Consent

You can withdraw your consent for data collection at any time by:

  • Opening the extension popup and toggling "Data Collection" off.
  • Uninstalling the extension from your browser.

Withdrawal is immediate. No further data will be collected or transmitted after withdrawal. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of data processed before the withdrawal.

12. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling as defined in GDPR Article 22. The bias scores displayed in the extension are for visual research purposes only and have no impact on the search results you see or any decisions made about you.

13. Security Measures

  • All data transmissions use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Server access is restricted to authorized research personnel.
  • The extension follows Chrome's Manifest V3 security model (no remote code execution, enforced content security policy).
  • The extension requests only minimal permissions (storage and alarms).
